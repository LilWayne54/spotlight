Edit rules

Use edit rules

Free: edit anything. Strike: finished sentences can't be edited, but you can strike one out (the S button) and write a new version. Strikes are permanent (you get 5 seconds to undo a slip). Alternate: like Strike, but the old version stays as a dotted alternate and your new one goes right after it. Use the S button on either one later to keep it and drop the other. Both treat any stretch of text as a sentence, period or not. Forward: no backspacing or going back in, even on the sentence you're writing. Just keep typing. Locked: finished sentences can't change. A sentence counts as finished once you start the next one.

Edit points

Writing earns a color points, up to its max. Deleting costs one point per character removed, and typing into the middle of a sentence costs one per character. When a color runs out, its text can't change until you write more in that color. The count shows on each light.

Sentence timers

A gentle check-in, not a lock. When you start a sentence in a timed color, a fuse starts burning. If it runs out before you start the next sentence, you hear a soft chime: a nudge to ask whether you're still writing or just polishing. Nothing gets locked.
